# NNNN — The decision, stated as a claim someone could disagree with

**Status:** accepted · **Code:** `the/file/that/implements/it.ts`

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two paragraphs a reader can act on without reading further. State it as a rule, not a history: *"enumerated columns are `String`"*, never *"we moved away from enums"*.

## Why

The reasons, and the alternatives that lost — each one named, with the reason it lost. A measurement beats an argument: give the number, what produced it, and what it reversed if it reversed a guess.

## Consequences

- What this makes true, what it costs, and what it rules out — the things a later reader would otherwise have to rediscover.
- Each bullet leads with its claim in bold, then the detail.
