# IT Support

## VPN

Remote access uses the **GlobalConnect** client. Install it from the software
portal, sign in with your work account, and approve the push notification.

Error **VPN-0x8007** means your device **certificate** has expired. Re-enrol the
device from the software portal; no ticket is needed.

## Passwords

Passwords can be changed at any time from the **self-service** password portal.
Support cannot reset a password on your behalf without identity verification.

## Laptops

Laptops are replaced on a **three years** cycle. Every device carries an asset
tag in the format **AC-** followed by six digits, printed on the underside.

A device that fails while you are travelling should be reported to the service
desk, which will arrange a courier replacement to your hotel or a loan unit from
the nearest office.

## New starters

Collect your laptop and asset tag from IT Support during your first week, then
enrol the device for VPN access.
