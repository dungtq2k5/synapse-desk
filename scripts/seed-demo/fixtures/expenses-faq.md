# Expenses FAQ

## How long do I have to claim?

Submit a claim within 30 days of the purchase, with the receipt attached.

## What is covered?

Travel, meals with clients, and home-office equipment up to the yearly
allowance of 450. The allowance resets on 1 January.

## Who approves a claim?

Your manager approves it, then Finance pays it in the next payroll run.
