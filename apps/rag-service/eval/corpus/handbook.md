# Employee Handbook

## Annual leave

Employees accrue 25 days of annual leave per calendar year. Unused leave may be
carried over into the following year up to a maximum of **5 days**; anything
beyond that is forfeited on 31 December.

Requests must be submitted at least **two weeks** in advance using form
**HR-22** (Annual Leave Request). Your line manager approves or declines within
three working days.

Annual leave may be taken during probation, but the accrual is pro-rated and
requests are limited to five consecutive days until probation ends.

## Probation

New employees serve a probation period of **six months** from their start date.
Probation may be extended once, by up to three months, with written notice.

## Sick leave

Notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A doctor's note is
required for absences of **three consecutive** working days or more.

## Your first week

New starters should complete the induction checklist, submit form HR-22 only if
leave is already booked, and collect their equipment from IT Support.
