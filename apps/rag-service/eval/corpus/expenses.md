# Travel and Expenses — policy P-4471

Policy **P-4471** covers business travel, subsistence and reimbursable
equipment for all employees.

## Meals and subsistence

The daily meal allowance while travelling on business is **45** per day
(domestic) and 60 per day (international). Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for any single expense above **25**. Below that
threshold a line on the claim form is sufficient.

## Booking travel

Flights and rail must be booked through the corporate travel portal. Travel is
booked to cost centre **CC-1180**. Claims for travel booked outside the portal
require director approval and are reimbursed at the portal's fare, not the fare
paid.

## Reimbursement

Submit claims within 30 days of the expense date. Approved claims are paid with
the following month's salary.

## Home-office equipment

A monitor, keyboard and chair may be expensed once every three years up to a
combined 500. Laptops are issued by IT Support and must not be expensed.
